#4471 is two problems, not one

owner support desk · open · reviewed 2026-11-02 · signature in source frontmatter

The #4471 page says one issue and one fix queued. That is wrong. The reports merged into #4471 hold two problems with two different causes. The queued fix (the parser) cannot fix one of them.

Not "known, one fix". The parser fix covers most reports, not all. One problem has no ticket of its own in the ticket system. It is filed as issue 2 in this repo.

The two problems

  1. Parser reads dates month first (issue 1). A day-first tenant gets day and month flipped. The fix is queued. The parser row below.
  2. The booking-v3 form hints MM/DD/YYYY (issue 2). Tenants that write the day first type what the hint says, and the form reads it their way. The parser fix does not help. The hint row.

How to tell them apart

Do not use the intake tag. It is a guess from the reporter's browser and is wrong for dozens of reports. Look up the tenant: its locale tells you whether it writes the day first, and its booking form tells you which hint its people saw. form at a booking-v3 tenant is hint.

Lessons

  • A big ticket is not always one bug reported many times. Merging on the subject line hid two problems under one.
  • Count once per problem, not once per ticket.
